Interactive Physics for Windows

The award-winning physics simulation software for the classroom. Observe, discover and explore the physical world, from the most basic topics to complex ones in STEM education.

Draw a model, press Run, and measure what happens. If you can use a mouse, you can use Interactive Physics. See how NASA uses it in its teacher development program.

What students and teachers can do

Build models

Draw circles, blocks and polygons, and add ropes, springs, dampers, pulleys, slots, actuators and motors.

Measure and graph

Velocity, acceleration, force, momentum and energy, in metric or English units, as numbers, graphs and animated vectors.

Experiment

Vary gravity, air resistance, friction or any material property, and see its effect on nearly any measurable quantity.

Collisions and sound

Simulate contact, collisions and friction. Hear and measure sound volume, frequency and Doppler effects.

Teach with the library

The Curriculum models with their workbook, and the Physics Experiments, ready to run and change.

Scripts and links

Desktop extras: scripts, the External Application Link, and presentations with graphics attached to objects.

Desktop or web?

Discovery oriented

Interactive Physics is a powerful tool for discovery learning. It develops inquiry skills and physics knowledge by letting students vary nearly any physical parameter, such as gravity, force, speed or spring constants, and measure its effect on nearly any measurable quantity, such as position, energy or decibel level.
